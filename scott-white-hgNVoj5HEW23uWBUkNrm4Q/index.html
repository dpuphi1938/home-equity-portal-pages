Homeowner Resources

Homestead Deduction
If you haven't already, file a Homestead Deduction with your county auditor — it isn't automatic and can reduce your taxable assessed value by the lesser of 60% or $48,000. A one-time filing, and easy to forget after closing.
Review your homeowner's insurance
Worth a quick annual check-in with your insurance agent, especially as your home's value changes — make sure your dwelling coverage still reflects what it would actually cost to rebuild, not just the market value shown above.
Property tax assessment
Your county reassesses periodically, and assessed value doesn't always track the real market — if a new assessment looks off relative to your home's actual value, you have the right to appeal it with the county assessor.
